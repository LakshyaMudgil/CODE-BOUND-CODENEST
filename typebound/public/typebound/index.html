HOW TO PLAY

THE LOOP

Type attack words to strike. Watch the boss telegraph, then dodge or parry. Break its poise to stagger it, then land a riposte for massive damage.

TYPING = YOUR BLADE

A word glows at the bottom. Type it exactly. Long words are heavy attacks. 100% accuracy + high combo = critical hits.

COMBAT KEYS

  • ← dodge left
  • → dodge right
  • ↓ dodge back
  • Shift parry
  • Space overdrive (when focus is full)

STAMINA

Dodging, parrying and attacking drain stamina. If it empties, you can't act — back off and let it recover. Manage it or die.

PARRY WINDOW

When the boss glows and a ring closes in, press Shift at the right instant. A perfect parry staggers the boss and opens a riposte.

DEBUG

F3 toggle debug overlay. F4 heal · F5 hurt boss · F6 stagger boss · F7 fill focus.
